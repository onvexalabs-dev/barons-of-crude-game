Where the GDPR or comparable law applies, processing necessary to provide requested multiplayer or support services is based on performance of a contract or steps you request before a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Handling technical security or abuse issues may rely on legitimate interests in protecting the service (Article 6(1)(f)); you may object to such processing where applicable. Legal obligations may require retention of certain correspondence (Article 6(1)(c)). Advertising-related processing is described in section 4; consent-based processing relies on your applicable privacy choices. The game makes no automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

4. Optional rewarded advertising

Ad-enabled builds use Google AdMob (Mobile Ads SDK 25.5.0) and User Messaging Platform (UMP 4.0.0). No ads open automatically. You may choose to check availability and watch one rewarded ad for 10,000 units of non-redeemable in-game cash. All gameplay remains available without watching ads. The app requests consent information when you request the ad feature and checks whether ads may be requested before initializing/loading ads.

Google's advertising SDK may process your IP address and approximate location inferred from it; app/ad interactions; performance and diagnostic information; and device/account identifiers, including advertising and app-set IDs where available. These support advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Ad data is sent to Google and its disclosed advertising providers, rather than a developer-run analytics server. No additional mediation SDK is configured in this version. Google documents encrypted transport and its data handling in the SDK disclosure and Google's privacy policy.

Where consent is required, the UMP form provides the applicable purposes, providers and choices before eligible ad requests. Choosing not to watch ads does not restrict play. Open Campaign Menu > Optional reward (or Ad preferences when hidden) to hide optional ads or access Ad privacy when UMP requires that entry point. Hiding ads prevents new optional requests; it does not itself withdraw previously given consent. Use Ad privacy to change available consent choices and Android settings to manage the advertising ID.

Reward receipts, timestamps and limits are saved locally to prevent duplicate rewards and restore earned rewards after interruptions. They are not sent to a publisher-operated server. Ad-provider retention and international processing are governed by the provider's disclosed policies; clearing local app storage does not erase records already held by those providers. The intended audience and territory-specific configuration must be finalized before production activation. Contact the publisher for questions or requests.

5. Website hosting and links

If you visit these pages through GitHub Pages, GitHub receives technical request information such as your IP address and browser details to host and secure the site. See the GitHub privacy statement. These pages set no cookies themselves, contain no analytics scripts, and load no external fonts. External links and email services have their own privacy practices.

6. Retention and deletion

Local game saves remain until you overwrite them, clear app storage, or uninstall the game. The export configuration disables Android app-data backup. Copies you or other players keep on their own devices cannot be remotely erased by the developer. Support correspondence is kept only as long as necessary to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations; you can request deletion by email. See deletion instructions.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location and the law that applies, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to certain processing. You can withdraw consent for future consent-based processing without affecting earlier lawful processing. You may also complain to the competent data-protection authority, including in your place of residence or work. Contact the developer using the address above. We may ask for proportionate verification before releasing or deleting personal information.

8. Children

This industrial management game is intended for a general audience and is not designed specifically for children. The developer does not knowingly solicit children's personal information. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent personal information can contact the developer. The final Play age-rating and audience declarations must reflect the released build; this statement does not override those declarations.

9. Security and international processing

The game uses Android's app-private storage. No method of storage or communication is perfectly secure. Multiplayer is intended for trusted local networks. Email and hosting providers may process information outside your country under their own published safeguards. Do not send passwords, financial credentials or unnecessary sensitive information in a support request.

10. Policy changes

The date above identifies this policy version. Material changes to data handling will be reflected here and, where appropriate, communicated in the app before the new processing starts.
