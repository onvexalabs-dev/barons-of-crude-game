Privacy policy

This policy covers the Barons of Crude game and this support website. The game is intended for players aged 13 and older. You can play without an account and without watching advertisements.

2. Local play and saved progress

Company selections, finances, fields, market history, settings and progress are stored on your device to run and resume the game. There is no developer-hosted account or gameplay-analytics server. Configured Android versions offer Google Play Games cloud saves as described below. Fictional money and coordinates are game data, not your financial information or location. Android app-data backup is disabled in the supplied build.

The game does not request access to your contacts, microphone, camera or precise device location. Its Internet access is used for Google Play Games, optional advertising, privacy choices and network functionality.

2a. Google Play Games and cloud saves

Configured Android versions use Google Play Games Services. Returning profiles may authenticate automatically; new players can select Connect in Cloud Saves. Campaign progress and training badges are stored with Google to resume on another device using the same Play Games account. The game uses your player ID to bind the local save and displays your Play Games name. Cloud saving has no payment or advertising requirement.

Pause Cloud stops the game's save synchronization, retaining existing data. It does not sign you out of Google or disable the platform SDK. Advertising consent, age and audio preferences remain local. Google's SDK collects analytics and diagnostics for its stability and improvement; Google processes gamer identity when profiles are created or updated. See Google's Play Games SDK disclosures and privacy policy. The game does not request your friends list or upload save screenshots.

Delete this game's Google Play data through your Play Games profile using Google's deletion instructions. Pause cloud saving on all devices first to prevent another local copy from being uploaded again. Uninstalling or clearing local storage does not delete the cloud copy. See our deletion and restore guidance.

3. Local-network multiplayer

If you choose LAN play, devices exchange IP addresses, connection information and game state directly with the host and connected players. This is necessary to connect and synchronize the session. The developer does not operate a matchmaking server or receive the session on a server. LAN traffic is not end-to-end encrypted; play only on a trusted network. Other players control any copies on their devices.

4. Optional advertising and age choice

Ad-enabled Android versions use Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Advertising is optional and restricted to players who locally select the 18-or-older age group. The game stores only an age category, not your date of birth. Players under 18, players who prefer not to state their age, and players who skip the age choice can keep playing without ad requests. You can correct this setting in Ad preferences.

Eligible adults can choose to check ad availability, then separately choose to watch a rewarded ad for $10,000 in non-redeemable in-game cash. There are no automatic interstitials or banner ads. Ads require a connection and availability is not guaranteed. All normal gameplay is available without them. Test builds use Google's sample ads; live ads remain disabled until publisher configuration is complete.

When an eligible player requests the ad feature, Google may collect or receive:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • Device or other identifiers, including advertising identifiers where available.
  • App and advertising interactions, such as ad views and taps.
  • Diagnostics and performance information, such as crashes and loading performance.

Google uses this information for advertising, measurement, analytics, fraud prevention and security. These data may be shared with Google's advertising partners as described in its notices. No additional mediation adapters are configured in this version. See Google's SDK data disclosure, Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from partner apps.

Where required, UMP presents the applicable consent choices before eligible ad requests. The app checks whether requests are permitted. Returning adult ad users may have their consent status refreshed without an ad opening. Refusing or withdrawing consent does not restrict normal play; depending on the available choices, ads may be limited or unavailable.

Open Campaign Menu → Optional reward or Ad preferences to hide optional ads, change your age category, or open Ad privacy when Google requires that entry point. Hiding the offer or changing age category stops new ad preparation, but does not erase provider records or itself withdraw an earlier consent. Use Ad privacy to change available consent choices. You can also manage your advertising ID in Android settings.

Reward receipts, timestamps and usage limits are saved locally to prevent duplicate rewards and recover earned rewards after interruption. They are not sent to a developer-operated server.

5. Support correspondence

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and resolve the issue. Mail is handled through Google's Gmail infrastructure. Do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary sensitive information. Attachments are optional.

This site itself sets no cookies and uses no analytics scripts, embedded ads or external fonts. Following an external link or sending email engages the relevant provider's separate services.

9. Retention and deletion

Local data remains until overwritten, cleared or removed by uninstalling the app. Clearing storage removes progress, age choices, ad settings and local receipts. It does not delete Google Play cloud saves or data already received by an advertising provider, correspondence in a mailbox or another player's independent copy.

Support correspondence is retained for as long as needed to resolve your request and meet applicable legal obligations, then deleted or anonymized. You can request deletion by email. Advertising and hosting providers apply their own retention periods, as described in their policies. See practical deletion instructions.

Subject to the applicable legal conditions, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability or object to processing. You may withdraw consent without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing. Contact us at the address above; proportionate verification may be needed before we disclose or delete information. We do not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects based solely on automated processing.

You may complain to a competent supervisory authority, including the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Datenschutzbehörde), or the authority in your habitual residence or place of work.

11. Younger players

The game is intended for ages 13 and above, not for children under 13. Optional advertising is restricted to adults as described above. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children. Parents or guardians can contact us if a child has sent personal information to support. The intended audience is distinct from a store's independently assigned content rating.

12. Security and updates

Local game data uses Android's app-private storage. Google Play Games and advertising SDK network transfers use encryption in transit according to Google's disclosure; LAN game traffic does not provide end-to-end encryption. No storage or communication system is completely secure.

The date above identifies the current policy. Material changes will be reflected here and, where appropriate, in the game before new processing begins.
