The world runs on oil.
Make it run on your terms.

Build a fictional energy company from the ground up. Acquire concessions, drill through difficult strata, connect pipelines and navigate an unpredictable market. Compete against AI rivals or friends in hotseat and local-network play.

Barons of Crude is an independently developed game by Manfred Uschan. All corporations and events in the game are fictional.

Development release. Version 0.5.0 includes optional rewarded ads. Development builds use test ads; production monetization awaits audience, consent and publisher setup. The full game remains playable without watching ads.
