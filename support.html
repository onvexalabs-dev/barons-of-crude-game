Support & data deletion

Get help

Email onvexalabs@gmail.com with “Barons of Crude” in the subject. Include the app version, Android version, device model, and steps that reproduce the issue. Screenshots are optional; remove personal information first. Never send passwords, signing keys or advertising credentials.

Delete local game data

  1. Open Android Settings and locate Apps → Barons of Crude.
  2. Choose Storage (the wording varies by device).
  3. Choose Clear storage / Clear data, or uninstall the game.

This permanently removes local campaign progress and settings. There is no game account or developer-hosted cloud save to delete. Starting a new campaign also overwrites the campaign save. Other players' independent copies are controlled by those players.

Delete support correspondence

Send a request from the address used to contact support, identifying the correspondence you want deleted. We will review and respond in accordance with applicable law and explain if a legal obligation requires limited retention.

Advertising choices

Ad-enabled versions offer optional rewards to adults. Age categories are stored locally and can be changed through Age setting in the reward/preferences panel. Open Campaign Menu → Optional reward (or Ad preferences) to hide the offer, or choose Ad privacy when that control is available to change consent choices. Device advertising controls can also be managed in Android settings. Deleting a game save does not necessarily delete information processed by a separate advertising provider.

Network troubleshooting

For local multiplayer, use the same trusted network, enter the host's local IP address, and allow UDP port 19890 in the host firewall. Some guest Wi-Fi networks prevent devices from communicating. This version has no public matchmaking or internet relay.
