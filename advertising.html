Optional rewarded ads

The full game is playable without watching ads. Ad-enabled versions let adults choose to watch one rewarded ad for $10,000 in-game cash. The currency has no real-world value and cannot be redeemed.

  • No banners, automatic ads or drilling interruptions.
  • Solo campaigns only; no ad advantages in LAN or hotseat games.
  • At most three rewards per UTC day, at least three minutes apart and one per three completed turns.
  • Closing an ad before earning its reward causes no gameplay penalty.
  • The offer can be hidden in Ad preferences.

An age category is stored only locally. Under-18 and unspecified-age players can play without requesting ads. Adults are shown applicable privacy choices before eligible ad requests.

Development builds use Google test ads. Live availability depends on publisher setup, consent, connection and ad supply. See the privacy policy for data practices and choices.
